Many findings, woven into one answer.

The evidence on peacebuilding rarely agrees with itself. Peace Intel draws the studies into a single position, keeps the disagreements in view, and leaves every thread attached to its source.

01 · Weft

Each finding enters as a thread.

One question, many studies. Full text runs solid, abstracts run hollow, and anything from the web runs dashed.

02 · Tension

Where the studies agree, the weave is tight. Where they conflict, it pulls.

Disagreement is information. It stays in the cloth.

03 · Bind

The answer is the thread that binds them.

One position in plain language, drawn through every finding. The tension eases and does not disappear, because the answer says where the evidence is still contested.

Every claim in the answer traces back to the study it rests on. Point at the cloth to lift a thread.

The draft

Written down before it is woven.

Weavers record a cloth as a draft: threads across the top, passes down the side, a filled square wherever they cross. An answer can be written the same way. Claims run down the side, sources across the top, and a mark sits wherever a claim rests on a source.

Illustrative: one answer, eight claims, twelve sources. Solid is held in full, hollow is an abstract, dashed is the web.
