The gap

The evidence exists. It rarely reaches the decision.

Decisions about where to intervene are made on deadline, far from the evaluations that could inform them. Peace Intel brings over 5,000 curated sources to the moment the choice is made.

C3 Method · .scrolly--full + vmap stills
How it works

From a question to a brief you can hand on.

Three moves, each of them visible, so you can see where every part of the answer came from.

01

Ask about a context

Name the place, the problem and the decision in front of you. Plain language is enough. Peace Intel finds the cases in the corpus that most resemble it.

02

See the evidence compared

Findings are set side by side across studies. It follows citation chains and says plainly whether the evidence is strong, mixed or untested.

03

Take away a brief

The answer comes back as a document you can read, check and hand to a colleague or a funder, with every claim cited to the passage.

C4 Tiers · grid-3 raised cards
How to read a citation

A gradient of trust, stated visually.

●1

Ingested

The full text is in the corpus. The citation points at the passage, and you can read the surrounding argument.

◐2

Abstract

The abstract is held and the full text is not. Enough to place a finding, not enough to verify how it was reached.

↗3

Web

Found outside the corpus. Deliberately carries no accent colour, because it has not been through the same scrutiny.
