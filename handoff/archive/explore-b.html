B2 The gap · prose + marginal
I · The gap

Where the evidence stops short.

Decades of evaluations, field studies and policy analysis describe what has reduced violence and what has not.

Most of it is hard to reach at the moment it would help. It lives in long reports and in the archives of the organisations that commissioned it, and the person drafting a programme or weighing a portfolio rarely has the week it would take to read it.

Peace Intel holds over 5,000 curated papers, evaluations and policy analyses, fully parsed rather than catalogued by abstract. Its research agent finds, compares and applies what the literature already knows, and it names the passage behind every claim.

A caveat worth stating plainly: a research agent can find and compare what has been written. It cannot tell you what to do. That judgement stays with you, and the citations are there so you can make it.
