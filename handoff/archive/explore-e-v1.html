01 · A question

What happened in Darfur between 2003 and 2011? Who intervened, what did they do, and what resulted?

The field goes quiet. Nothing has been retrieved yet.

02 · It reads

The passages that bear on it come forward.

Every source is fully parsed, so retrieval works on what a study says rather than on its title. Related work sits close together, which is why the answer arrives in clusters.

03 · It compares

Gathered, ranked and set side by side.

It follows citation chains between them, and separates what agrees from what conflicts.

04 · Three carry it

Solid, hollow, dashed.

Each citation wears its tier. One came from outside the field, and it arrives marked.

05 · The answer

Three interventions, three different failures.

The African Union deployed AMIS in 2004 with a monitoring mandate it was never resourced to fulfil ●1. Its successor UNAMID was the largest peacekeeping operation in the world at its peak, yet its civilian protection record remains contested ◐2.

A frequently cited displacement figure does not trace to a primary source in this corpus ↗3, so treat it with caution.
