Pl. 03TextureThe dashed grid
Rare, or it stops being a fingerprint.

The grid used to run under the whole page. Wallpaper flattens everything and leaves nothing for a textured area to contrast against. Between 2003 and 2011 the conflict in Darfur drew three distinct intervention efforts, and they are worth separating because they failed for different reasons.

The African Union deployed AMIS in 2004 with a monitoring mandate it was never resourced to fulfil ●1. Its successor UNAMID, authorised in 2007, was the largest peacekeeping operation in the world at its peak, yet its civilian protection record remains contested ◐2. Parallel mediation in Abuja produced an agreement in 2006 that most rebel factions declined to sign ●1.

Pl. 09Media hierarchyFour tiers, descending
One loud thing per page.

Four tiers of image and motion. At most one tier-one element per page, and never two tiers competing in the same viewport. Without the rule a page ends up with a hero video, a section video and a grid texture all fighting, which reads as noise rather than atmosphere.

Tier 1

Tier 2

Dithered loop behind a section. One per band.

Tier 3

Figure

A still diagram, chart or specimen. Framed, opaque.

Tier 4

Grid vignette

The dashed texture. Sparing.

All motion assets share one treatment: medium cell, seven percent mono Bayer dither, greyscale, multiply blend. That treatment is the brand's image language, so a new asset that is not dithered will look like it came from somewhere else. On dark surfaces the blend switches to screen, because multiply would erase the asset entirely.

The dither was picked in July because it looked good. It turns out to be the product metaphor as well — an image resolving out of noise — which is why the motion language in the next plate is built from it.

The fivewhat each one costs you
Warm

The current value. Warm, tactile, distinctly not a SaaS grey. Costs contrast headroom: the ink ramp had to be re-solved against it.

Deep

More separation, more warmth. Reads richer and heavier. Needs its own ink ramp to hold AA, which this file provides.

Wash

A neutral derived from the ink rather than its own hue. Tracks the theme automatically. Loses the temperature.

Flat

No fill. Elevation alone separates it. Lighter and more architectural; the warmth goes entirely.

Hairline

No fill, no shadow, pure drawn line. The most editorial and the flattest. Everything rests on the quality of the rule.

Components read three tokens rather than assuming a fill exists: --panel, --panel-border and --panel-elev. That is what lets the treatment change globally. Anything that hardcodes var(--panel) as a background and adds its own border will not follow the switch.

Worth checking this one in the console rather than on the marketing page. The chat bubble is the highest-frequency raised surface in the product by a long way, and it is the place a wrong choice will grate.

Pl. 12Don'tMeasured, not hypothetical
Every one of these actually happened.

These are failure modes found in this codebase, not cautionary inventions.

Don't invent type sizes

index.html uses 19 whole-pixel sizes. rotary-a.html grew to 25 including 9.5 and 14.5px; problems.html to 32.

Don't write off-grid spacing

24 distinct vertical margin values in one page. Every space is a --sp-* step.

Don't hardcode white on dark

rgba(255,255,255,.6) and #fff across .ctaband, .close and .card. Use a surface context.

Don't fork the palette per page

Production sets brown at the base and two page files override to orange, so the live site renders two brands by route.

Don't wallpaper the grid

It was under the entire page. Texture that is everywhere is texture nowhere.

Don't put a translucent card on texture

The dashes read through the face as dirt. Every surface in this system is opaque.

Don't re-declare a shared rule to change it

All three section{padding} values came from re-declaring the rule after the block. Edit the block.

Don't give one class two meanings

.chip was a solid pill in four files and bare text in two. .cta was a button in one lineage and a section in another.

Don't use the accent decoratively

It marks evidence, primary action, focus and active state. More than three or four accented spots means it is over-accented.

Don't reach for the typewriter

Terminal cursors, redaction bars and scanlines are costume. The motion language is resolution, not performance.
