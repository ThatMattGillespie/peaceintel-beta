H3 Versus · plain comparison table
A fair question

How is this different from ChatGPT?

General assistants are remarkable at a great many things. Peace Intel is built for one of them: answering peacebuilding questions from evidence you can check.

A general AI assistant Peace Intel
What it reads A broad mix of training data, and the open web when it searches. A curated library of over 5,000 peacebuilding papers, evaluations and policy analyses, read in full.
Who chose the sources Nobody chose them for this field. The Effective Peacebuilding Initiative and the Alliance for Peacebuilding.
How it cites Sometimes links to web pages. Every claim to the passage it came from, with the source’s tier on its face.
When the evidence is thin Usually answers fluently anyway. Says the evidence is mixed or untested, and names the gap.
A number it cannot trace States it like any other fact. Flags it before you repeat it.
Built for Everyone, and everything. Peacebuilding decisions: funders, implementers and policy teams.

Describes general-purpose assistants as they are commonly used. Features vary by product and version.

The partnership

Built with the field it serves.

Peace Intel is a project of the Effective Peacebuilding Initiative and the Alliance for Peacebuilding, the network that connects organisations working for peace around the world. Its library is assembled and curated by both, from the field’s own evaluations, studies and policy analysis.

Why it matters

A research tool is only as good as the library behind it, and this one was chosen by people who do the work.
