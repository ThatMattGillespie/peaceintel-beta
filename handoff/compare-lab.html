Peace Intel

The evidence supports a narrower claim than this.

I can’t confirm it as stated. Contact can change how people treat those they actually meet●1●2, and a wide review finds it generally improves attitudes, with weaker effects on ethnic and racial prejudice●3.

It does not show an effect on violence. In Nigeria, shared classes reduced discrimination without reducing prejudice●1. In Iraq, behaviour improved among teammates, with little spillover beyond the team●2.

Before Friday

Which groups, where, and what kind of contact? Is there recent violence between them? That decides which of this evidence applies.
