Console parts, on the system.

For Nathanael and his coding agent. Production asks clarifying questions before deep research and streams research updates while it works; the console prototype drew neither. Both are now in components.css. Each specimen below is the markup to build, so read this page’s source. The rules are in AGENTS.md, the console’s behaviour in CONSOLE.md (section 8, H1.19 to H1.21), and the CSS wins over both.

What was wrong

The accent was on nine things in two views, where a view gets three or four, and only on things you act on. Corners were rounded past 4px. The serif was set on every line, where a card gets one serif line.

What changed

Face and edge for the card, 4px corners from --r-*. One serif line, the question. The options sit in a tray. The updates are written in ink, folded under one mono line. The only accent left is the tint button that moves you on.

What it costs

CSS and markup only: the data and the behaviour production already has. One wording change: the duplicate “Something else (please specify)” option is dropped, since “Write my own answer” does the same job.

H1.19Clarifying questionsStable · .clarify
Ask before the research, quietly.

Built the way the session reflection is built: a raised object in the thread (face, edge, 4px). The count is a neutral mono eyebrow, because it names a place, not a state. Where you are is the 2px ink line and the steps to come are hairlines. The question is the one serif line. The options are a choice among options, so they sit in a tray, and the chosen one rises out of it as a face tile with an ink dot. With the keyboard, the option in focus shows it by its own edge in the middle grey: no orange ring on a choice. Next is the tint; Skip and Back are quiet.

H1.23 · H1.24 · H1.25An artifact’s toolbar, and editing a spreadsheetStable · Rev 62 · 23 Oct 2026
Edit in the shape you read.

Every control in a toolbar is 32px (--sp-8), icons, mono actions and buttons alike. The pencil renames in place, at the title’s size. Edit table starts editing; the table keeps its reading layout and the cell being edited becomes a field in place, with a field’s focus: an ink edge and a lift. H1.21The input field’s active stateProduction does not match
The field you type in lifts. It does not light up.

The active state of production’s input field does not match the design that was handed off. Build it from the CSS, not from what production draws now.

The composer

No shadow at rest. While you type in it, its edge darkens to --ink-35 and it lifts to --elev-3 (.composer:focus-within). The textarea inside draws nothing of its own: no ring, no border, no padding, no shadow. The composer is the box.

Every other field

Focused, a text field’s edge darkens to --ink-35 and it takes --elev-1, with no outline (.input:focus). That covers the “Write my own answer” field above, the reflection’s field and the dialogs.

Never

An accent ring or an accent edge on a field. The accent’s focus ring (:focus-visible) is for buttons and other controls reached by keyboard, not for a field you are typing in.
