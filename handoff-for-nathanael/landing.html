Three interventions, three different failures.

Three distinct efforts, worth separating because they failed for different reasons. The African Union deployed AMIS in 2004 with a monitoring mandate it was never resourced to fulfil ●1. Its successor UNAMID was the largest peacekeeping operation in the world at its peak, yet its civilian protection record remains contested ◐2.

A frequently cited displacement figure does not trace to a primary source in this corpus ↗3, so treat it with caution.

How to read a citation

A gradient of trust, stated visually.

Not every source is equally strong, and a system that hides that is asking to be trusted rather than earning it. Every citation carries its tier on its face.

●1

Ingested

The full text is in the corpus. The citation points at the passage, and you can read the surrounding argument.

◐2

Abstract

The abstract is held and the full text is not. Enough to place a finding, not enough to verify how it was reached.

↗3

Web

Found outside the corpus. Deliberately carries no accent colour at all, because it has not been through the same scrutiny.

The tell

Solid, hollow, dashed. The shape carries the meaning, so the tiers survive a colourblind reader, a greyscale print and any future change to the palette.
