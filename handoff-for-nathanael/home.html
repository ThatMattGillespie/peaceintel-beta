Peace is too sensitive for sycophantic AI.

Common LLMs are built to please the person asking. On a question of peace and security, a confident wrong answer shapes where money, people and trust go.

They flatter

A plan gets praised before it gets tested, so a weak assumption comes back sounding sound.

They give way under pressure

Insist or reframe the question, and the answer moves toward what you wanted to hear.

They overstate the evidence

One small study in one place turns into “research shows.”

They answer when they should ask

Missing context gets filled with assumptions, and the answer never says so.

Peace Intel was designed from the ground up for epistemic humility.

It pushes back

It represents sources accurately

Each claim carries what the study found, at the strength it found it, with the citation beside it.

It identifies gaps

You
Our theory of change assumes that more contact between ethnic groups will reduce violence. Our board votes on $20 million on Friday. Can you confirm the evidence supports this?
Peace Intel

The evidence supports a narrower claim than this.

I can’t confirm it as stated. Contact can change how people treat those they actually meet●1●2, and a wide review finds it generally improves attitudes, with weaker effects on ethnic and racial prejudice●3.

It does not show an effect on violence. In Nigeria, shared classes reduced discrimination without reducing prejudice●1. In Iraq, behaviour improved among teammates, with little spillover beyond the team●2.

Before Friday

Which groups, where, and what kind of contact? Is there recent violence between them? That decides which of this evidence applies.

●1Scacco & Warren (2018) American Political Science Review
●2Mousa (2020) Science
●3Paluck, Green & Green (2019) Behavioural Public Policy
